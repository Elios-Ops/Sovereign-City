The Lore of MuskRats.io — Sewer Prophecy

📜 Origins of the Sewer

Deep beneath the city, far from the algorithmic gaze of the surface world, lies the OpenSewer. A glitch in the grid. A sanctuary for those born from chaos. This is where the MuskRats emerged—creatures of satire, survival, and subversion.

🧬 The Glyphborn & Bloodborn

The Glyphborn trace their ancestry to the first glitch—a burst of corrupted code that cracked the Treasury’s firewalls. In contrast, the Bloodborn are forged from sacrificial lore and ancient sewer rites. One born from digital decay, the other from ritual mutation.

🧔 The Don’s Rise

Once a sewer thief, The Don witnessed the BloodMoon Sacrifice and was marked by a Rune. That Rune, hung around his neck, transformed him from a gutter ghost into the ruler of the underground. He formed The F.A.M.—the Financial Ascent Mob—to protect the prophecy.

🌀 Codex & Prophecy

The Codex Scrolls document events yet to come. They whisper riddles, reveal vaults, and grant access to bounties. The MuskRats follow the prophecy: "From the sewer to the stars… till the cheese drips off these walls."
